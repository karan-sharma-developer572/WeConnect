Problem Statement

About WeConnect

Dementia is a progressive brain condition that affects memory, thinking, and the ability to carry out everyday activities. With millions of people affected worldwide, it is becoming a growing concern for older adults, families, and caregivers, highlighting the urgent need for better care, safety, and support.

The challenge

Dementia slowly affects memory and orientation. Seniors can forget routines, medicines or the way home, and families worry when they can't be nearby.

Our approach

WeConnect combines real-time location tracking, safety alerts and gentle reminders, so seniors stay independent while caregivers stay informed.

What it means for families

Peace of mind for relatives, dignity for seniors, and one shared place where caregivers can coordinate care.

Solution

Our Services

We believe that growing older should never mean losing independence or feeling disconnected. Our platform brings elderly people, family members and doctors together through one simple application.From medication reminders and mental wellness activities to emergency assistance and recovery monitoring, WeConnect provides reliable support while keeping families informed and connected.
